    Tips

    Mini guides, updated regularly

    What to save

    As the end of the season approaches, we should each set realistic goals to prepare for the next one. By saving the right resources now, you'll start S3 with explosive momentum.

    • Protect at all costs: Sand, Ore, Gold, and Battle Essence.
    • Okay to use: Fantamon trinkets, for pulls.
    • Avoid using: Stellatie.
    • Gacha events: If an event has pull requirements, use affection tokens to meet them.

    Destiny fruits

    Destiny fruits are something every player should look forward to! They're a great chance to finish a relic set and secure a nice set bonus.

    The game has a pre-determined location for every relic (see the guide below). Once you decide whether to pull for blue or purple relics, just go to that location and use your 10 fruits.

    • Timing: Save them for events like Bingo, where you benefit slightly more from using them.
    • Be ready for anything: You could pull almost anything with Destiny fruits. If you plan to use 250-300 fruits, try to have a strong Dawnium reserve of 8,000-10,000.
    • Buy everything: Progression is time-gated, so buying it all speeds things up and gives you a nice discount.
    • Stay realistic: Relic drop chances are still quite low, even for blues. Be patient and stick to your goals. Hunting for legendary or mythic relics is not advised.

    Vendor

    This is where you'll spend your gold. Some of you may be struggling to keep up with gold or to afford your next upgrade. Luckily, we have a very simple trick.

    Here's the standard we follow:

    • Top row: Always buy it all out. No explanation needed.
    • Everything else: Only buy it if it's on sale for 20% or more.
    • Refreshes: We recommend 3-4 refreshes per day if you want to save rolla and start the next day with more.

    You'll run out of gold quickly if you try to buy everything. If you spend responsibly, you'll watch your rolla grow instead of shrink! And once you know what you'll need for upcoming upgrades, you can do extra refreshes on the shop.
